# Product & Project Vision

This document details the vision, philosophy, and motivations behind Yuva Samrajya's core offerings.

---

## 💼 Zekkers: Human Capability Accelerations

Zekkers acts as a launching pad for students and job seekers across India.

### 🌟 Problem

Traditional hiring models rely on static, outdated, and unverified resumes. Students struggle to map coordinate skills to market-ready standards, and local employers struggle to audit verification checks.

### 🌟 Solution

Zekkers builds automatic assessment pipelines:

1. **Interactive Pathfinding**: Auto-generate personalized study courses using real-time skill gaps.
2. **Genkit-Verified Profiles**: Direct PDF uploads map candidate parameters to Firestore, instantly matching target profiles.
3. **Decentralized Rewards**: Tokenize engagement, motivating and rewarding early-career developers.

## 🕉️ Malola: Elevating Spiritual Well-Being

Malola provides a voice-first, community-centered digital spiritual home.

### 🌟 Problem

Spiritual exploration, astrology calculations, and scripture learnings are fractured across disjointed platforms, which are often filled with distracting advertisements.

### 🌟 Solution

Malola unifies modern UX conventions with spiritual science:

1. **AI Astrology engine**: Uses precise astro calculations to output chart readings, transits, and daily reflections.
2. **Centralized Media Vault**: Fast PDFs, audio playback, and script transcribing for learners.
3. **Inclusive Marketplace**: Seamless support for temple directories and flower/deity item delivery.
